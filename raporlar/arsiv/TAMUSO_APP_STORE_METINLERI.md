## 1. Açıklama — English (App Store "Description")

<!-- ACIKLAMA_EN_BASLANGIC -->
Tamuso is a social app for adults (18+) to meet new people, go live, join voice rooms and talk with friends, all in one place.

LIVE STREAMING
• Go live with your camera in a few taps and chat with your viewers in real time.
• Watch creators, follow the ones you like and see when they are live.
• Join friendly PK battles between two live streams.
• Save short clips from a live stream and share them as a story.
• Live chat can be translated into your language.

VOICE ROOMS
• Create a voice room on any topic or join an existing one.
• Take a seat to speak, or simply listen.
• Hosts manage seats, moderators and room settings.

MESSAGES AND CALLS
• Private messages with photos, videos, voice notes and music.
• One-to-one voice and video calls.
• Discover people and call them. Some users set a per-minute Coin rate for calls. The rate and your balance are always shown before the call starts, and the call begins only after you confirm.

POSTS AND STORIES
• Share posts and 24-hour stories with photos and videos.
• Like, comment and send gifts on stories.

COINS AND GIFTS
• Coins are a virtual item purchased through In-App Purchase.
• Use Coins to send animated gifts in live streams, voice rooms, messages and stories, and to pay for calls.
• Coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

MY WALLET
My Wallet shows three separate sections, each with a short explanation:
• Purchased Coins: Coins you bought through In-App Purchase.
• Game Coins: Coins won in games. They cannot be purchased or withdrawn.
• Creator Earnings: the earnings of eligible creators, shown in US dollars.

CREATOR EARNINGS
• Eligible creators receive 40% of the shareable revenue from gifts and paid calls made with purchased Coins. Tamuso keeps 60%.
• Every earning shows its source, amount and status.
• Payouts are made by bank transfer after identity verification. The minimum payout is $25.00 and payout periods are 15 days.
• Gifts sent with Game Coins or reward Coins do not create earnings.

FRUIT WHEEL
• A social mini game played with Coins in rounds with other players.
• Prizes are Game Coins only. There are no real-money or real-world prizes.
• The odds and expected return of every fruit are shown in the game rules.
• Results are random and decided on our servers. Nobody can choose the result of a round.

AGENCIES
• Creators can join an agency that supports them with events and guidance.
• Agencies have their own profile, members and meeting rooms.

SAFETY AND CONTROL
• Tamuso is only for users aged 18 and over.
• Report or block any user, message, post or live stream.
• Live hosts and room moderators can mute or remove participants.
• Our team reviews reports and suspicious activity.
• Privacy settings let you choose who can find you, call you and see your activity.
• You can delete your account at any time from Settings.

AVAILABLE IN
English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, French and Filipino.

## 3. App Review Notes — English (App Review Information → Notes)

<!-- REVIEW_NOTU_BASLANGIC -->
Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing Tamuso. This version addresses Guidelines 2.5.19 and 5.6 from our previous submission.

1. WHAT THIS BUILD CONTAINS
All user-facing screens in this build are reachable by any signed-in user. No screen is unlocked by a hidden gesture, a special build, a country setting, or a reviewer-only flag, and no code is downloaded at runtime.
This build contains: live audio rooms, one-to-one calls, messaging, virtual gifts, Coins, and one casual game (Fruit Wheel).
Not present in this build: AI Music Studio, the staff/moderation console, and every other game engine. Their routes do not exist in the binary; deep links to them resolve to "not found".
Coins are sold inside the iOS app only through Apple In-App Purchase. The website has its own checkout for web users and is not linked from the iOS app. Agency (B2B) Coin sales are handled outside the app and are not purchasable in the iOS app.
Feature flags in this build can only pause a feature that is already shipped (for example, to pause gifts during an incident); they cannot enable anything that is not in the binary.
The staff/moderation console is a separate web application at a different domain. It is not part of this binary, no staff screen or staff route exists in it, and the iOS app contains no link, button, or deep link that opens it. Staff balance adjustments are audit-logged and do not change game odds or outcomes.

2. COINS (IN-APP PURCHASE)
Coins are consumable In-App Purchases and are sold on iOS only through Apple In-App Purchase. Each transaction is verified on our server with the App Store Server API and credited once. There are no external payment links. Coins have no cash value.

3. MY WALLET
Three separate sections, each with an explanation:
- Purchased Coins: bought through IAP.
- Game Coins: won in games. Cannot be purchased or withdrawn. Gifts sent with them create no earnings.
- Creator Earnings: in USD, for eligible creators.

4. CREATOR EARNINGS
Creators receive 40% of the shareable revenue from gifts and paid calls made with purchased Coins; Tamuso keeps 60%. Agency commissions are paid from Tamuso's share, not the creator's. The Wallet shows creator earnings in USD for transparency.
Payout requests are not available in this version. The Wallet states this explicitly and no request form is reachable.

5. PAID CALLS
Users may set a per-minute Coin rate for one-to-one calls. The rate and the caller's balance are shown before the call, and the call starts only after the caller confirms.

6. FRUIT WHEEL (SIMULATED GAMBLING)
Fruit Wheel is the only game in this build. It is played with Coins, and prizes are Game Coins only: no cash value, no withdrawal, no real-world prizes. The odds and expected return are shown in the rules screen. Results are random on the server and cannot be set by anyone. The app is 18+ and the age rating declares simulated gambling.

7. SAFETY
18+ only: a date of birth is required at registration and under-18 dates are rejected by the server. Users can report and block users, messages, posts and live streams. Hosts and moderators can mute or remove participants. Reports are reviewed by our team. Account deletion: Profile > Settings > Delete Account.

The account is a normal user (no staff rights) with 100 bonus Coins from the welcome bonus, so gifts and Fruit Wheel can be tested without a purchase. To test In-App Purchase, please use a Sandbox Apple ID; the purchase flow is the standard StoreKit sheet.

## 4. Gönderimden önce doğru olması gerekenler

Bu metinler aşağıdakiler tamamlanınca doğrudur. Biri eksikse ilgili cümle çıkarılmalı veya düzeltilmelidir.

1. Ekonomi migration'ları (085900, 090000–090600) ve edge function'lar canlıya alınmış olmalı. Aksi hâlde "üç ayrı bölüm", "%40", "oyun kazancı Oyun Coin'i" ve "admin sonucu belirleyemez" ifadeleri canlı sistemle uyuşmaz.
2. Meyve Çarkı bugün canlıda `PLATFORM_APPROVED_COIN` modunda: bahis satın alınan Coin bakiyesinden düşüyor. Bu yüzden App Store Connect yaş derecelendirmesinde "Simulated Gambling" sorusuna **"Frequent/Intense"** cevabı önerilir; uygulama zaten 18+.
3. `notification-push` edge function'ı canlıda bozuk (dakikada bir 500). "Canlı olduklarında gör" ifadesi uygulama içi listeye dayanır, ancak bildirimler düzeltilmeden önce test edilmelidir.
4. Para çekme durumu: review sırasında kapalıysa 4. maddedeki kapalı seçeneği kullanılmalı ve **cüzdanda çekim formu görünmemelidir**. Şu an kod `withdrawals_enabled=true` iken "çekilebilir" yazıyor ama `cekim_talebi_olustur` yetkisi kaldırılmış durumda; bu hâliyle not yazılırsa 5.6 ihlali olur. Çekim kararı verilmeden gönderim yapılmamalıdır.
5. Demo hesap, ekran yolları, Kullanım Koşulları / Gizlilik Politikası adresleri ve iletişim bilgileri doldurulmalı.
6. Açıklamada fiyat, "ücretsiz coin", "para kazan", "kumar", "bahis", "jackpot", Android veya başka platform adı geçmemeli (mevcut metinde yok).
7. 1. maddede "personel konsolu bu binary'de yok" deniyor. Bunun doğru olması için `app/admin/**` **iOS bundle'ından çıkarılmış** olmalı; aksi hâlde bu cümle silinmelidir. Doğrulama: `strings main.jsbundle | grep admin/` boş dönmeli. Web paneli ayrı uygulama olarak `apps/super-admin` altında çalışır.
8. 1. maddede "diğer oyun motorları binary'de yok" deniyor. Bunun için `app/oyun/` altında yalnızca `fruit-wheel*.tsx` kalmalı; zeus, nox, kaskad, fair-spin, astral-falls, sis-spin, dede, storm-king dosyaları kaldırılmış olmalı.
